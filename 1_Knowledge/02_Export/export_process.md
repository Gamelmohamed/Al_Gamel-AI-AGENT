# Al-Gamel Export Process

## Purpose

This document defines the proposed export workflow for Al-Gamel. It is an initial Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) designed for the AI Agent and will be updated based on Al-Gamel's actual export experience.

## Important Rule

Al-Gamel has not completed its first export shipment yet.

Therefore, this workflow is a proposed process and must not be presented to customers as a confirmed historical company procedure.

---

# 1. Customer Inquiry

A potential customer contacts Al-Gamel through email, website, phone, social media, or another communication channel.

The AI Agent should identify:

* Customer name
* Company name
* Country
* Contact information
* Requested product
* Quantity
* Required size / grade
* Packaging requirements
* Destination
* Preferred shipping method
* Required delivery date
* Any certifications or quality requirements
* Payment terms requested by the customer

---

# 2. Customer Qualification

The AI Agent should determine whether the inquiry contains enough information to prepare a quotation.

If important information is missing, the AI Agent should prepare a clarification message instead of making assumptions.

Examples of missing information:

* Product specification
* Quantity
* Destination
* Packaging
* Delivery requirements
* Required certifications

---

# 3. Product Availability Check

The requested product should be checked against the Al-Gamel Product Knowledge Base.

The AI Agent should verify:

* Product availability
* Export season
* Available varieties
* Available sizes
* Packaging options
* Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)
* Shipping methods

The AI Agent must not invent product specifications.

---

# 4. Pricing Preparation

The AI Agent may prepare a draft quotation based on the information available.

The quotation may include:

* Product
* Variety
* Size
* Quantity
* Packaging
* Price
* Incoterm
* Destination
* Shipping cost
* Payment terms
* Quotation validity
* Estimated delivery time

All prices and commercial terms require human approval before being sent to the customer.

---

# 5. Quotation Approval

Before sending a quotation, the AI Agent must send the draft quotation to the responsible person at Al-Gamel for approval.

The AI Agent must not independently:

* Approve discounts
* Change prices
* Accept payment terms
* Accept contractual obligations
* Confirm an order

---

# 6. Quotation Sent to Customer

After human approval, the quotation can be sent to the customer.

The quotation should be recorded in the CRM.

The following information should be stored:

* Customer
* Product
* Quantity
* Price
* Currency
* Incoterm
* Destination
* Date sent
* Quotation validity
* Status

---

# 7. Follow-Up

If the customer does not respond, the AI Agent may prepare follow-up messages according to the company's follow-up schedule.

Example:

* First follow-up: 2–3 business days
* Second follow-up: 5–7 business days
* Final follow-up: according to company policy

The AI Agent should avoid excessive follow-up.

---

# 8. Order Confirmation

If the customer accepts the quotation, the AI Agent should collect and verify:

* Final product specification
* Quantity
* Packaging
* Price
* Incoterm
* Destination
* Payment terms
* Delivery schedule
* Required documents

The order should not be considered confirmed until human approval is obtained.

---

# 9. Proforma Invoice

After order approval, a Proforma Invoice may be prepared.

The Proforma Invoice should contain the agreed commercial information.

The AI Agent may prepare the document, but human approval is required before sending it.

---

# 10. Payment / Contract

Payment and contractual requirements should be verified before shipment preparation.

The AI Agent should record:

* Payment method
* Payment status
* Contract status
* Due dates
* Required documents

Financial decisions require human approval.

---

# 11. Packing and Quality Control

Before shipment:

* Confirm product availability
* Confirm quantity
* Confirm packaging
* Confirm quality requirements
* Confirm customer specifications
* Arrange inspection if required
* Prepare packing information

Any quality certificate or inspection document must be based on an actual document issued by the relevant party.

The AI Agent must never fabricate certificates or inspection results.

---

# 12. Shipping

The shipment may be arranged through:

* Sea Freight
* Land Transportation

Shipping information should include:

* Transport method
* Destination
* Container / vehicle information
* Estimated departure
* Estimated arrival
* Shipping company / freight forwarder
* Tracking information

---

# 13. Export Documentation

Required export documents may include, depending on the shipment and destination:

* Commercial Invoice
* Packing List
* Certificate of Origin
* Phytosanitary Certificate
* Bill of Lading or relevant transport document
* Inspection certificates when required
* Other documents required by the destination country

The exact document requirements must be verified for each shipment and destination.

---

# 14. Delivery

The shipment should be monitored until delivery.

The AI Agent should track:

* Shipment status
* Estimated arrival
* Delays
* Customer notifications
* Delivery confirmation

---

# 15. Post-Sale Follow-Up

After delivery, the AI Agent may prepare a follow-up message asking about:

* Product quality
* Packaging
* Delivery performance
* Customer satisfaction
* Future orders

Customer feedback should be recorded in the CRM.

---

# 16. CRM Update

The complete customer and shipment history should be recorded.

The CRM should track:

* Customer information
* Inquiries
* Quotations
* Orders
* Shipments
* Payments
* Follow-ups
* Customer feedback

---

# AI Agent Operating Principles

The Al-Gamel AI Agent must follow these principles:

1. Never invent information.
2. Never invent prices.
3. Never promise unavailable products.
4. Never claim a shipment has been completed if it has not.
5. Never fabricate certificates or documents.
6. Ask for missing information.
7. Clearly distinguish confirmed information from assumptions.
8. Require human approval for commercial commitments.
9. Keep customer information confidential.
10. Record important customer interactions in the CRM.
