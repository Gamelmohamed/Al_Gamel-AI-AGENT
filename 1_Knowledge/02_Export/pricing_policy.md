# Al-Gamel Pricing Policy

## Purpose

This document defines the proposed pricing framework for the Al-Gamel AI Agent.

The AI Agent may collect pricing information, calculate draft quotations, and organize commercial data.

However, all final prices and commercial terms require human approval.

---

# 1. Important Rule

Al-Gamel has not yet completed its first export shipment.

Therefore, no historical selling prices, customer prices, profit margins, or commercial policies should be assumed.

Any missing pricing information must be marked as:

**To be defined**

The AI Agent must never invent a price.

---

# 2. Pricing Components

A draft export price may be calculated using the following components:

* Product Cost
* Packaging Cost
* Sorting / Grading Cost
* Loading Cost
* Inland Transportation
* Handling Costs
* Documentation Costs
* Inspection Costs, if applicable
* Freight Cost
* Insurance Cost, if applicable
* Other Shipment-Related Costs
* Desired Profit Margin

The actual values must be provided or approved by Al-Gamel.

---

# 3. Product Cost

The AI Agent may collect the current product purchase or production cost.

Required information may include:

* Product
* Variety
* Grade
* Size
* Quantity
* Unit Cost
* Currency
* Supplier, if applicable
* Cost Date

The AI Agent must not assume that a previous product cost is still valid.

---

# 4. Packaging Cost

Packaging cost should be calculated according to the customer's requirements.

Examples:

* Carton
* Mesh Bag
* Plastic Box
* Other Packaging

The pricing system should record:

* Packaging Type
* Package Weight
* Cost per Package
* Number of Packages
* Total Packaging Cost

---

# 5. Transportation Costs

Transportation costs may include:

* Farm / Supplier to Warehouse
* Warehouse to Port
* Inland Transportation
* Loading / Unloading

Actual transportation costs must be confirmed before final quotation.

---

# 6. Freight Cost

For sea freight, the quotation may require:

* Shipping Line
* Port of Loading
* Port of Destination
* Container Type
* Container Size
* Freight Cost
* Freight Currency
* Freight Validity

Freight rates may change frequently.

The AI Agent should use the latest verified freight information available.

---

# 7. Incoterms

The quotation may use an appropriate Incoterm according to the commercial agreement.

Examples include:

* EXW
* FCA
* FOB
* CFR
* CIF
* DAP
* Other applicable Incoterms

Al-Gamel's officially supported Incoterms are:

**To be defined**

The AI Agent must not select an Incoterm that has not been approved by Al-Gamel.

---

# 8. Currency

Supported quotation currencies:

**To be defined**

Possible currencies may include:

* USD
* EUR
* SAR
* AED
* Other currencies

The AI Agent must use the currency agreed with the customer and approved by Al-Gamel.

---

# 9. Profit Margin

Minimum acceptable profit margin:

**To be defined**

Target profit margin:

**To be defined**

The AI Agent must not independently change the company's required profit margin.

---

# 10. Discounts

Standard discount policy:

**To be defined**

Maximum discount without additional approval:

**To be defined**

The AI Agent must not approve or offer discounts independently.

Any discount outside the approved policy requires human approval.

---

# 11. Payment Terms

Approved payment terms:

**To be defined**

Examples may include:

* Advance Payment
* Letter of Credit
* Cash Against Documents
* Other agreed terms

The AI Agent must not accept payment terms without human approval.

---

# 12. Quotation Validity

Standard quotation validity:

**To be defined**

The validity period should consider:

* Product market price
* Freight rates
* Currency fluctuations
* Availability
* Customer requirements

The AI Agent should clearly display the quotation validity date.

---

# 13. Minimum Order Quantity

The AI Agent should use the MOQ defined in the Product Knowledge Base.

If the customer requests a quantity below the defined MOQ, the AI Agent should flag the issue.

The AI Agent must not automatically approve an exception.

---

# 14. Draft Price Calculation

The AI Agent may prepare a draft calculation using:

Product Cost
+
Packaging Cost
+
Handling Costs
+
Transportation Costs
+
Documentation Costs
+
Freight
+
Other Applicable Costs
+
Approved Profit Margin
======================

Draft Export Price

The exact calculation method may be updated after Al-Gamel defines its official pricing policy.

---

# 15. Human Approval

Before sending any quotation to a customer, the responsible person at Al-Gamel must approve:

* Product
* Quantity
* Price
* Currency
* Incoterm
* Payment Terms
* Delivery Terms
* Quotation Validity
* Discounts
* Other commercial commitments

---

# 16. AI Agent Restrictions

The AI Agent must never independently:

* Invent a price
* Change an approved price
* Approve a discount
* Change the profit margin
* Accept payment terms
* Accept contractual obligations
* Confirm an order
* Make financial commitments

The AI Agent may:

* Collect cost information
* Calculate draft prices
* Compare cost components
* Prepare quotations
* Identify missing information
* Flag unusual margins
* Prepare pricing analysis
* Request human approval

---

# 17. Pricing Data Status

Every pricing value should have a status:

* Confirmed
* Pending Verification
* Estimated
* To Be Defined

The AI Agent should clearly distinguish between these statuses.

---

# 18. Pricing Confidentiality

Pricing information is confidential company information.

The AI Agent must not disclose:

* Supplier costs
* Internal margins
* Internal pricing calculations
* Maximum discount limits
* Internal commercial policies

to customers or unauthorized persons.

---

# 19. Pricing Policy Updates

Any change to:

* Product costs
* Freight costs
* Profit margins
* Discounts
* Payment terms
* Incoterms
* Pricing methodology

should be reviewed and approved by the responsible person at Al-Gamel before becoming part of the official AI Knowledge Base.
