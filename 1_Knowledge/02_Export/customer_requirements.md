# Al-Gamel Customer Requirements

## Purpose

This document defines the information that should be collected from a potential customer before preparing an export quotation.

The AI Agent should use this checklist to identify missing information and ask the customer for clarification.

---

# 1. Customer Information

The AI Agent should collect:

* Customer Name
* Company Name
* Country
* City
* Email Address
* Phone Number
* Website
* Job Title / Position
* Preferred Contact Method

---

# 2. Product Requirements

The AI Agent should identify:

* Product
* Variety
* Quantity
* Required Size
* Quality / Grade
* Packaging
* Carton / Package Weight
* Special Product Requirements

---

# 3. Shipment Requirements

The AI Agent should collect:

* Destination Country
* Destination City
* Port of Destination, if applicable
* Preferred Shipping Method
* Required Delivery Date
* Number of Containers
* Required Container Type, if applicable

---

# 4. Commercial Requirements

Before preparing a quotation, the AI Agent should identify:

* Preferred Currency
* Preferred Incoterm
* Payment Terms
* Required Quotation Validity
* Target Price, if the customer provides one
* Any requested discount

---

# 5. Documentation Requirements

The AI Agent should ask whether the customer requires specific documents or certificates.

Examples:

* Certificate of Origin
* Phytosanitary Certificate
* Quality Certificate
* Inspection Certificate
* Other destination-specific documents

The AI Agent must not claim that a certificate is available unless it has been verified by Al-Gamel.

---

# 6. Customer Qualification

The AI Agent should identify:

* Is this a new customer?
* Is the customer an importer, distributor, wholesaler, retailer, or other type of business?
* What products does the customer normally import?
* What is the expected order frequency?
* Is this a one-time order or a recurring requirement?
* Has the customer imported Egyptian produce before?

---

# 7. Missing Information

If important information is missing, the AI Agent should not guess.

It should identify the missing information and prepare a concise clarification message.

Example:

If the customer requests:

"25 tons of Egyptian oranges to Saudi Arabia."

The AI Agent should identify that the following information may still be required:

* Preferred orange variety
* Required size
* Packaging
* Destination city
* Preferred Incoterm
* Payment terms
* Required delivery date

---

# 8. Minimum Information Before Quotation

The following information should normally be available before preparing a quotation:

* Customer / Company Name
* Product
* Quantity
* Destination
* Required packaging
* Required size or specification
* Shipping method
* Preferred Incoterm

If any critical information is missing, the AI Agent should request clarification before finalizing the quotation.

---

# 9. Human Approval

The AI Agent may collect and organize customer requirements.

However, final commercial decisions require human approval.

The AI Agent must not independently:

* Set or change prices
* Approve discounts
* Accept payment terms
* Accept contracts
* Confirm an order
* Make financial commitments
